# Small Space Planner — Content Roadmap

## Publishing principle
Small Space Planner should become a free learning library for people who live in studios and compact homes. Every page must solve a real decision. We do not publish unrelated trends or near-duplicate keyword pages.

Quality gate for every new article:
1. One clear reader problem and one clear search intent.
2. A direct answer near the top.
3. Original planning logic, checklist, decision framework, calculation, diagram, comparison or tool connection.
4. No invented experience, test result, statistic or professional credential.
5. Primary sources for safety, health, building-system or regulatory claims.
6. Server-rendered text, unique title/description, internal links and permanent URL.
7. Useful even if the reader arrived directly, not only from Google.
8. At least two relevant internal links and one next action.
9. Avoid publishing when the topic is already fully covered by an existing page.
10. Review the live build after publishing.

## Trend rule
Trends are for audience acquisition, especially social content. A trend becomes a website article only when the topic itself is genuinely useful to the existing small-space audience. Do not publish unrelated entertainment, politics, celebrity or finance stories on the site merely because they are popular.
