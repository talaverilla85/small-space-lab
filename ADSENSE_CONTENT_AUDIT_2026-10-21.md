# AdSense / Search content audit — 2026-10-21

Scope: the 32 active articles in `/learn`.

## Audit standard

Each article was reviewed against these risks:
- low or no added value;
- scaled content whose primary purpose is search manipulation;
- doorway-style near-duplicate pages;
- scraped or lightly rewritten third-party content;
- misleading affiliation, product claims or unsupported factual claims;
- weak user experience where ads could outweigh publisher content.

The site-level rule remains: an article must solve a real small-space problem and add first-party editorial value through measurements, decision logic, comparison, worked examples, checklists, original diagrams, tools, or useful synthesis of reliable sources.

## Trend-content watch list

These pages are acceptable because the article is useful beyond the news event, but future edits must preserve that:
- compact toilet launch → fixture projection and bathroom measuring method;
- IKEA retractable hooks → loaded-vs-empty storage footprint;
- IKEA floral textiles → pattern scale and visual-clutter decisions;
- holiday 2026 → circulation, storage and electrical-safety planning;
- warm minimalism → space-neutral texture and palette decisions.

Do not create a trend article when the only original contribution is a summary, reaction, rephrasing of a press release, or a weak connection to small-space living.

## Ongoing publishing gate

Before any new article is indexed:
1. Search the existing library for overlapping intent.
2. State the real reader problem in one sentence.
3. Require a unique practical contribution beyond prose.
4. Verify safety, health, regulatory, availability and product-specification claims with current primary sources.
5. Never imply testing, endorsement, affiliation or personal experience that did not occur.
6. Keep publisher content clearly more prominent than advertising.
7. Check internal links, canonical, structured data, title, description, date and images.
8. If the article is not materially useful after the trend fades, do not publish it as an SEO article.
