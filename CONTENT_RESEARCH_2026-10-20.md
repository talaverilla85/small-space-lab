# Editorial research — 2026-10-20

Two distinct, researched additions are planned for the Small Space Planner learning library.

## 1. Comparing two studio layouts
Proposed slug: /learn/compare-two-studio-apartment-layouts

Unique value: a 100-point scorecard using circulation (30), usable access (25), daily routines (20), storage (15), and daylight (10). Ratings are 0–5 and weighted points equal rating/5 × weight. Example: Plan A scores 71, Plan B scores 77. These are illustrative, not empirical. Related pages: how-to-measure-a-studio-apartment, how-to-zone-a-studio-apartment, furniture-measuring-checklist.

Research: https://www.ikea.com/es/en/rooms/living-room/how-to/very-small-living-room-ideas-to-maximise-your-space-pub655da950/

## 2. Vacuum storage in small apartments
Proposed slug: /learn/vacuum-storage-in-a-small-apartment

Unique value: compare cordless stick, canister and robot storage by static footprint, retrieval envelope, charging requirements, accessories and daily access. Include a worked 16-inch-gap versus 14-inch-head example, making clear that static fit does not guarantee easy retrieval.
